Pay Log

Every membership, ticket, and donation processed through AGMS is logged here. Use Refund to send a payment back to the card; charges still awaiting settlement are voided automatically.

✉ Send someone a payment link

For one-off charges you agreed on over the phone — valet sponsorship, a late ticket, a negotiated renewal. Name it, set the amount, and either copy the link or have the website email it. The amount is locked, so nobody can type in the wrong number.

▤ What people can pay for online

This is the drop-down on the public Make a payment page. Add whatever people call to pay for — breakfast tickets, name badges, renewals — so they pick it from a list instead of typing it and guessing. Leave the price blank when the amount varies (renewals) and the payer will be asked to enter it.
