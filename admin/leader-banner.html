Leader Level Banner

Every logo on the leader banner at the bottom of the website, in the order it appears there. A member joins this banner by having a Leader level set on their row in Members — Friend through Platinum.

The logos on the banner

Most of these carry a banner logo of their own, set when the site was built. That picture wins over the member’s profile logo, which is why updating a profile does not change the banner. Remove it and the member’s own logo takes over.
See the banner on the website ↗

Every logo is drawn the same height. Most of these came across from the old website already shrunk to a 100×60 thumbnail, and some hold only ten or twenty pixels of actual logo inside a lot of empty space. The website now crops each file to the logo itself and draws them all 44 pixels tall, so the width varies with the shape of the mark and the heights match. The pictures above are what the banner shows.

What that costs. A logo that only held 13 pixels is now blown up more than three times to reach that height, and it looks soft — there is no way around that but a better file. The Size on the page column says which ones those are; use Replace once the member sends you one. A logo that sits on its own coloured square or inside a printed border cannot be cropped any further, because that square is part of the picture.
