Hi Felicia,

Your directory questions, plus Diana's page and the Education Committee - all

1. Can Wendy find a member by key words, or does it have to be a category?

Key words, and she already could. Every member profile carries a set of search
keywords behind the scenes, and both Wendy and the directory search box read
them. Try "braces" in the directory search - you'll get the orthodontists, even
though not one of them has the word "braces" anywhere a visitor can see. Same
with "lodging" for the hotels.

So the category is not the only way in. It was the only way in for *browsing*,
which is what item 2 fixes.

2. Fewer categories.

The category picker now has two levels. It opens on twenty-one broad types -
Health & Medical, Restaurants & Food, Legal, Real Estate and so on - and the
full list of specific categories sits underneath them. Pick either level, and
the type-to-filter box still searches both. Members browsing get the short list
first; anyone hunting for "Orthodontist" specifically can still get there.
One of the twenty-one is brand new - see item 5.

3. Your spreadsheet is in.

Thank you for pulling it - the different column order was no trouble. 558 of the
607 businesses on the site matched a row in your export, and 71 of them now
carry two or three categories instead of one. That is the real win: a business
that caters *and* has a banquet hall used to be findable under only one of
those. Now it's under both.

An example you can check: filter the directory by "Catering". It used to return
one business. It now returns six - Cavaretta's, Chick-fil-A, Grandi Italiani,
Jerk Wings, Mort's and Safir. All five of the new ones were already members who
cater; they were filed as "Restaurant" or "Delicatessen", so a member looking
for a caterer never saw them.

The other 49 businesses had no matching row in your export, mostly small
spelling differences between the two lists. They kept the single category they
already had, so nothing was lost.

5. Fourteen listings were in the wrong section - now fixed.

Sorting your categories turned up a real bug on our side, and it's an amusing
one. The sorting was matching on letters rather than whole words. So:

- The Los Angeles Times, The Acorn, Valley News Group and Van Nuys News Press
  were all filed under Beauty & Personal Care - because "new-SPA-per" contains
  "spa".
- So were Barrister Executive Suites, Konnekted, W Executive Suites and Warner
  Center Executive Suites, because "Office SPACE Leasing" does too. They're
  under Real Estate now.
- A retirement planner sat under Automotive ("re-TIRE-ment"), three
  transportation companies under Fitness & Recreation ("tran-SPORT-ation"), an
  environmental consultant under Health & Medical ("environ-MENTAL"), and West
  Valley Nursery under Health & Medical as well ("NURSery").

All fourteen are in their right sections now. Beauty & Personal Care is down
from thirteen listings to the five that actually belong there.

While fixing it I also added a Media & Press section. Your twenty-three
