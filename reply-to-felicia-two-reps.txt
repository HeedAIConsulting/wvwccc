Hi Felicia,

Short answer: yes. Each representative gets their own sign-in - their own email and their own password - and both open the same business profile. So two admins can each keep the profile up to date without sharing a password.

Here's how you set it up, and it's live now:

1. Members > find the business > click the new gold "🔑 Logins" button. It shows everyone who can sign in on that account.

2. Type the second person's email in the box and click "Give them access". The website emails them a link to set their own password, and nothing changes for the first person - their sign-in stays exactly as it is.

That same box is also where you handle each person separately from then on: "See their view", sign-in link, reset link, and set-a-password all work per person now, so resetting one rep's password never locks out the other. And if someone leaves the business, "Remove" takes away just their sign-in - the profile and the other person's access stay put.

When a member row shows "🔑 Logins (2)", that's your at-a-glance sign the account has two reps on it.
