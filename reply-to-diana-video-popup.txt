Hi Diana,

Thank you for the go-ahead on the video. Here's how I'd like to put it to work, and then one bigger idea at the end.

1. Get it onto YouTube first.

Before it goes anywhere else, the finished cut should live on the Chamber's own YouTube channel. I don't believe the Chamber has one yet - the website links Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn but no YouTube - so if that's right, it's worth having and I'm glad to set it up and hand you the keys.

Then send me the link and I'll put it everywhere: the homepage, all three of your social accounts, the newsletter, the Chamber's Google Business Profile, the Join page, and staff email signatures.

Why YouTube rather than just uploading the file in each place: it's one link that works on every device, you keep the video and its view count as the Chamber's own asset, it costs nothing, and it gives the Chamber a library to grow into - mixers, breakfast recaps, member spotlights, the Gala. Every future video lands in the same place instead of scattering across platforms.

2. The homepage popup.

What I have in mind, and I want your okay before I build it: a welcome video that opens on its own the first time someone lands on the homepage. A clean panel over the page with the video, a Watch button and a clear X to close, and then it doesn't come back for that visitor.

The case for it: this is the best first impression the Chamber has, and someone deciding whether to join will watch sixty seconds of you and the community before they'll read a page of text. It's the closest thing we have to greeting someone at the door.

The case against, so you have both: popups interrupt, some people close them on reflex, and they wear out. My recommendation is we run it, with guardrails:

- It opens muted with captions on, so it never blares in an open office.
- It shows once per visitor, not every visit.
- Homepage only. It never appears when someone is paying, RSVPing or signing in.
- The video also sits on the homepage itself, below the hero, so anyone who closes it can still find it.
- We give it a month, then look at whether people actually watch. If they're closing it inside two seconds, the popup comes down and the on-page version stays.

One question: everyone, or only visitors who aren't signed-in members? My instinct is everyone for the first few weeks - it's as much a reminder to members of what their Chamber is as it is a pitch to newcomers.

If you'd rather skip the popup entirely, the alternative is the video near the top of the homepage, playing when someone scrolls to it. Less interruption, less attention. Happy either way, just tell me which.
