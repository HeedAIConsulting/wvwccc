Hi Diana,

Thank you for the go-ahead on the video - I'd like to put it to work right away.

Here's what I have in mind, and I want your okay before I build it: a welcome video that opens on its own the first time someone lands on the homepage. A clean panel over the page with the video, a Watch button and a clear X to close, and then it doesn't come back for that visitor. Anyone who has been to the site before wouldn't see it at all.

The case for it: this is the best first impression the Chamber has, and a visitor deciding whether to join will watch sixty seconds of you and the community before they'll read a page of text. It's the closest thing we have to greeting someone at the door.

The case against, so you have both: popups interrupt, and some people close them on reflex - a few will be annoyed no matter how well it's done. It's also the kind of thing that wears out. My recommendation is we run it, but with guardrails:

- It opens muted with captions on, so it never blares in an open office.
- It shows once per visitor, not on every visit.
- It never appears on a page someone landed on to do something specific - paying, RSVPing, signing in. Homepage only.
- We put the video on the homepage itself as well, below the hero, so anyone who closes the popup can still find it.
- We give it about a month and then look at whether people are actually watching. If they're closing it inside two seconds, we pull the popup and keep the on-page version.

One question for you: should it play for everyone, or only for visitors who aren't signed-in members? My instinct is everyone for the first few weeks, since the video is as much a "look what your Chamber is" reminder for members as it is a pitch to newcomers.

If you'd rather not have a popup at all, the alternative is putting the video right at the top of the homepage where it plays when someone scrolls to it - less interruption, less attention. Happy either way; just tell me which you want.

Once Donavan sends the final cut with the fixes, I'll have it up the same day. The vertical version he's making will be perfect for social, and the 20-second cut is the right length for the homepage if we decide the full one is too long to open with.
