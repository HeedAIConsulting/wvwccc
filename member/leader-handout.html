Managing Your Group on the Chamber Website

Five things every group leader, Connection Circle leader, and Ambassador can do on the website today. No forms to send the office. Everything below is on your own Manage group page.

⇩ Download the PDF

1Sign in and find your group

woodlandhillscc.net → Member sign-in

  • Use the email address the Chamber has on file for you.
  • No password? Choose Email me a sign-in link. A one-click link arrives in your inbox (check spam the first time).
  • Your dashboard opens with a green Group management card at the top. Click Manage group.

2Put your meetings on the calendar

Manage group → Add an event / Set up recurring meetings

  • Set up recurring meetings: pick the first date, uncheck any holiday, and create the next 3 to 12 months in one go.
  • The office gives a quick approval, then each meeting appears on the Chamber calendar showing Hosted by your group.
  • Edit fixes a date or typo. RSVPs shows who is coming, with email and phone.

3Approve members and build your roster

Manage group → Approve / Decline box, Add a member

  • When someone asks to join from your group's public page, you get an email and an Approve / Decline box at the top of your page.
  • Add members yourself: search the Chamber directory, or type a name and email for someone who is not a member yet.
  • Anyone you add with an email on file gets a welcome email automatically.

4Email the group, post notes and photos

Manage group → Email the group, Meeting notes, Photo albums

  • Email the group sends your message to every member individually. Replies come to you.
  • Tick Add the next meeting's details and the date, venue, and RSVP link are added for you.
  • Meeting notes you save appear on your group's public page right away. Create a photo album per meeting and members can add their own shots.

5Ambassadors: sign up for an event shift in one click

Member dashboard → Volunteer at an event

  • Every event that needs Ambassadors lists its open roles and how many people each still needs. Click a role to take it, and you can cancel from the same card.
  • Your name appears on the public Ambassadors page. For the photo, upload a square image, at least 400 × 400 px, under Edit my profile.
  • The office sees who covered what on every event, so there is no separate sign-up sheet to email around.

Members' own calendars

Every meeting page has Add to Calendar buttons for Google, Outlook, and Apple. Members tap once and the meeting is on their phone.

Stuck?

Use the Support button in the bottom-left corner of any page (screenshots help), or replay the walkthrough with Take a quick tour on your Manage group page.
