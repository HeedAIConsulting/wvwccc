Add your event to the Chamber calendar

Members post their own events on the Chamber's website: open houses, workshops, ribbon cuttings, anything the community is welcome to. The Chamber office approves each one and it goes on the calendar.

1 · Sign in

Go to woodlandhillscc.net and click Member Login. Use the email address the Chamber has on file for you. No password yet, or forgot it? Type your address under Email me a sign-in link and click Email me a link. A one-click link arrives in your inbox (check spam the first time).

2 · Start a new event

Your dashboard has a card called Your events on the calendar. Click ＋ Add an event on it.

If you also lead a Chamber group, a Posting as box at the top lets you choose between your business and the group.

3 · Fill in the details

  • Event name, Date and Category.
  • Start time, and an End time if you have one.
  • Location / venue, and the Address if people need it to find you.
  • Description: what it is and who it is for. The buttons above the box make text bold, add a picture, or turn words into a link with 🔗 Link (for example “Click here to register” instead of a long web address).

4 · The optional parts

  • Repeats? Choose Every week or Monthly (e.g. first Monday) for something on a schedule, and every date is created in one go. Leave it on One time otherwise.
  • RSVP button: choose Add an RSVP button and put your address in Email RSVPs to. Each RSVP is emailed to you as it comes in.
  • Flyer: upload an image of your flyer and it shows on the event's page.

5 · Send it

Click Add to calendar. The event goes to the Chamber office, and it appears on the calendar once they approve it. Until then it sits on your dashboard marked awaiting the office.

6 · Changing it later

Every event you post is listed on the Your events on the calendar card. Edit changes one. Duplicate runs it again on a new date and leaves the original where it is.

Editing an event that is already on the calendar sends the change to the office too, and the event comes off the calendar until they approve it.

🛟 When you're stuck

Use the Support button in the bottom-left corner of any page. Your note goes straight to the team that runs this website, and screenshots help. Prefer a person? Call the Chamber office at (818) 347-4737.
