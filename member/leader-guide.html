Group Leader Guide

Everything a group or Connection Circle leader does on the Chamber website, in one place. Keep it open the first few times — after that you won't need it.

1 · Signing in

Go to woodlandhillscc.net → Member sign-in, and use the email address the Chamber has on file for you. No password yet, or forgot it? Choose “Email me a sign-in link” — a one-click link arrives in your inbox (check spam the first time). Once you're in, your dashboard shows a green Group management card at the very top.

2 · Your group's home base

Click Manage group on that card. Everything below happens on this one page:

  • ＋ Add an event — one click sends it to the Chamber office, and it appears on the calendar right after their quick approval, showing “Hosted by” your group on your group's public page.
  • ⟳ Set up recurring meetings — meet the first Monday of every month? Pick the first date, confirm the generated list (uncheck a holiday), and the next 3–12 months are created in one go. Each date stays its own event, so you can still change a venue later.
  • Edit / Remove on any event — fix a date, a typo, or take one down. Your changes go live immediately.
  • RSVPs — see exactly who's coming and how many people they're bringing, with their email and phone.

3 · Members & join requests

When someone asks to join from your group's public page, an Approve / Decline box appears at the top of your management page (you're also emailed). You can add members yourself — search the Chamber directory, or type in a name and email for someone who isn't a member yet. Anyone you add with an email on file gets an automatic welcome email explaining the group page, RSVPs, and photos — you don't have to send one. (No email on file? The page tells you, so you can collect one.)

4 · Messaging the group

Click 📣 Email the group — write a subject and message, and it goes to every member on your list, each person emailed individually. Tick “Add the next meeting's details” and the date, time, venue, and an RSVP link are appended automatically. Replies come straight to you.

5 · Meeting notes & photos

Meeting notes — type agendas, recaps, or announcements into the Meeting notes box and Save; they appear on your group's public page (newest at the top reads best). Photo albums — create one per meeting or event; it shows on your group page, and any signed-in member can add their own shots from the album itself, so you never have to collect photos by email.

6 · Calendars for your members

Nothing to set up — and nothing in the admin. This is already on every meeting page on the public website. It's here so you can tell a member how to use it when they ask.

Members can put your meetings on their own personal calendar (the one on their phone or computer) so they get reminded automatically. Here's how it works:

  • Open any meeting on the website — from your group's page or the Events calendar.
  • Near the bottom of that page is a small ADD TO CALENDAR line with three buttons: Google, Outlook, and Apple / .ics.
  • They tap the one that matches the calendar they use: Google for Gmail/Google Calendar, Outlook for Outlook.com, and Apple / .ics on an iPhone, iPad, or Mac (it opens right in their Calendar app).
  • That's it — the meeting is on their calendar with the date, time, and location filled in.

For monthly meetings: each month's meeting is its own page on the website, so a member adds September's meeting from September's page, October's from October's, and so on — one tap per date, adding only the ones they plan to attend.

7 · Your own profile

Your business listing is what the public sees — keep it current under Edit my profile. For the photo on the Leaders/Ambassadors pages, upload a square image, at least 400×400 px (a headshot or square logo). Wide rectangular logos get cropped, and small images turn blurry when enlarged.

🛟 When you're stuck

Use the Support button in the bottom-left corner of any page — your note goes straight to the team that runs this website, and screenshots help. Prefer a person? Call the Chamber office at (818) 347-4737. And the “Take a quick tour” button on your management page replays the walkthrough any time.
