Ambassador Guide

How to sign up for a job at an event, and how to record everything you do away from one.

1 · Signing in

Go to woodlandhillscc.net → Member sign-in, and use the email address the Chamber has on file for you. No password yet, or forgot it? Choose “Email me a sign-in link” — a one-click link arrives in your inbox (check spam the first time).

Your dashboard has an Ambassadors card. That is the tracker. If you cannot see it, the Chamber office has not marked you as an Ambassador yet — ask them and it appears next time you sign in.

2 · Volunteering for a job at an event

Under Volunteer at an event you will see the upcoming events that need help, with the jobs still open on each one — registration, greeter, setup, and so on. Click a job and you are down for it; the office sees immediately who is covering what.

Changed your mind? Your own sign-ups are listed with a Cancel button. Do it as early as you can so somebody else can pick the job up.

Nothing listed? Then no upcoming event has asked for volunteers yet. It is not you — check back.

3 · Logging what you did away from an event

Plenty of what an Ambassador does never happens at an event — a post you shared, a review you left for a member business, a Buddy check-in, a business you referred. Use Log something you did on the same card:

  • Pick what kind of contribution it was (the five below).
  • Set when — today is filled in, change it if it was earlier.
  • Write what you did in a line, and add any detail worth keeping.
  • Add to my tracker. It shows up under “What you've logged”, and the office sees it.

Log it as you go rather than saving it all up — a line written the same week is far more useful than a list at the end of the year.

4 · The five kinds of contribution

These describe the type and effort of the contribution — not the value of the person. You are not working up a ladder; most Ambassadors do a bit of several. The right-hand column is the detail worth writing down.

TierWhat it coversWorth noting
1 · Connect & PromoteSocial media posts, shares, check-ins and engagement; help member businesses; provide reviews for member businesses.Posts/engagement • businesses helped • reviews provided
2 · Represent & EngageServe as an Exhibit Table Representative; attend and help at ribbon cuttings; assist at breakfasts, mixers and other monthly events.Events represented • ribbon cuttings • breakfasts/mixers
3 · Serve at EventsHelp at annual and special events as a host; assist with registration/sign-in; help with auctions and other event assignments.Event • role • hours/shift • impact
4 · Hands-on Event SupportPack and load materials from the Chamber office; transport; set up; break down; load vehicles; return to the office; unload and put everything back in place.Specific tasks completed • event • time commitment
5 · Grow & Retain — Member TLCBuddy System: check in, welcome, introduce, encourage participation, help members use benefits and feel connected. Submit a Buddy Report. Refer new businesses for membership.Buddy TLC • Buddy Report • member engagement/retention • new members referred

Tiers 2, 3 and 4 usually happen at an event, so you will often be signing up for those in step 2 rather than logging them afterwards. Tiers 1 and 5 almost never do — those are the ones to log.

5 · Points

A job you sign up for at an event carries its points with it. Anything you log yourself starts at zero — the Chamber office reads it and decides what it is worth, so give it a day or two before you look for it to change.

You cannot set your own points, and that is deliberate: it keeps the tracker something the office can stand behind.
