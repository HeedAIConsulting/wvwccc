Privacy Policy

Last updated July 2026 · DRAFT — legal review required before launch.

The West Valley · Warner Center Chamber of Commerce respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly — such as your name, email, phone, and business details when you join, register for an event, donate, or contact us. Payment card data is processed securely by our payment provider (AGMS) and is never stored on our servers.

How we use it

To operate the directory, process memberships, events, and donations, send receipts and chamber communications, and respond to your inquiries. We do not sell your personal information.

Analytics and session recording

We use Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use this site — which pages get read, how people move through the directory, and where they run into trouble. Both set cookies and record general activity such as pages viewed, clicks, scrolling, referring site, device type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. Microsoft Clarity also captures heatmaps and anonymized replays of page interactions; it masks text entered into form fields by default, and neither tool receives payment card data — that goes only to our payment provider.

We use this only to improve the website. You can opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, and most browsers let you block or clear cookies. See Google's privacy policy and Microsoft's privacy statement for how each provider handles what it collects.

Your choices

You may update your member profile or unsubscribe from communications at any time. Contact (818) 347-4737 or message us with any privacy request.
