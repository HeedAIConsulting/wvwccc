# Creative Brief · AI in Action Live

**Brand:** Heed AI Consulting. Voice: direct, measured, zero hype. Every claim is a time, revenue, or compliance lever with a number, or it does not ship.

## Positioning

* The market is saturated with AI theory and prompt tips. Almost nobody shows working systems that real businesses run. The whole campaign hangs on one promise: **you will watch three real client builds run live, with scrubbed data, and see exactly what each changed.**
* Title: **AI in Action: Three Real Client Builds, Shown Live**
* Format: LinkedIn LIVE, Wednesday August 12, 11:00 AM Pacific, 45 minutes. Agenda: 5 minute framing, three 10 minute demos, 10 minutes of Q and A.

## The three demos and their levers

* **DTC dashboard** (public label: The Owner Dashboard). A direct to consumer command center pulling orders, ad spend, and fulfillment into one live view. Lever: time. Placeholder: [X hours per week] of reporting eliminated.
* **SJ doors project** (public label: The Doors Project). Intake, quoting, and job tracking for a custom door builder, rebuilt around AI. Lever: revenue velocity. Placeholder: quote turnaround from [X days] to [X hours].
* **Alpha Structure site information and risk assessment tool** (public label: Site Intelligence and Risk). Turns site data into a structured risk picture a crew can act on. Lever: compliance and risk. Placeholder: assessment prep from [X] to [X].

Public copy uses the descriptive labels. Michael decides on stage whether to name clients. All demos are scrubbed builds, and the copy says so plainly, which is itself a trust signal.

## Audience

* Owners, partners, and operations leaders at 10 to 500 person companies in retail and ecommerce, manufacturing and trades, and construction and engineering. United States, weighted to Southern California.
* Pain: they have heard AI pitches for two years and seen nothing concrete. They buy demonstrations, not decks.

## Creative rules (enforced)

* Bright and clear imagery, warm cream, amber, and sage palette. No blue backgrounds, no dark corporate gradients.
* All images generated with no text, no typography, no logos. Copy and logos go on in overlay only.
* No hyphens or dashes anywhere in copy, captions, hashtags, or file names.
* No inflated AI language: no autonomous, no fully automated, no guaranteed. What runs on screen is the claim.
* Every destination URL carries UTM tags on campaign `ai_in_action_live_2026_08`.

## Angles in play

* Proof over promise: "stop reading about AI, watch it run" (primary, the boost post)
* Behind the build: what each system looked like before, one vivid detail per project
* Countdown urgency: day before and day of posts
* Replay capture: the follow up asset keeps collecting leads after the event ends
