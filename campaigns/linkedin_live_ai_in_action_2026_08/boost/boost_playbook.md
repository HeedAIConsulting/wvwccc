# Boost Playbook · AI in Action Live

This is the complete spec for the browser connect session where the boosted post and audience get built in LinkedIn. Everything is decided here so that session is execution only.

## Guardrail

No spend moves without Michael's explicit go in the LinkedIn interface. This document recommends, it does not push.

## The post to boost

Post B in `social/linkedin_posts.md`, published on the **Heed AI Consulting company page** on Wednesday August 6 with `images/hero_16x9.png` attached. Boost the published post rather than creating a dark ad, so the engagement stays public on the page.

Destination: **the LinkedIn Event page** (LINKEDIN_EVENT_URL). Registration is native on LinkedIn, one tap from the boosted post to Attend.

## Objective

Boost objective: **event attendance** (boost the event or the Post B that carries the event link, whichever the boost flow offers). Lead capture into HubSpot happens by downloading the event registrant list, see `../linkedin/event_setup.md`. Track boost performance in LinkedIn's boost analytics rather than UTMs, since registration never leaves LinkedIn.

## Audience spec (build exactly this, then save as "AI in Action Live 2026 08")

* Location: United States, plus a 50 mile radius around Los Angeles as a second saved audience for a 30 percent budget split
* Age: 25 and older
* Company size: 11 to 500 employees
* Seniority: Owner, Partner, CXO, VP, Director
* Job functions: Operations, Information Technology, Engineering, Business Development, General Management
* Industries: Construction, Building Materials, Retail, Consumer Goods, Manufacturing, Real Estate, Machinery
* Interests, if the boost flow offers them: Artificial Intelligence, Business Process Improvement
* Exclusions: employees of Heed AI Consulting

Keep the estimated audience between roughly 50,000 and 300,000. Below 50,000 delivery chokes, above 300,000 relevance dilutes. Trim industries first if oversized, never seniority.

## Budget and pacing

* Total: **$300 to $500** across August 6 through August 12, ending at 11:00 AM Pacific on event day
* Split: 70 percent national audience, 30 percent Los Angeles radius
* Expected LinkedIn CPM: $30 to $60, so 8,000 to 15,000 impressions
* Working math at a 1 percent click rate and a 40 percent page conversion: 80 to 150 clicks, 30 to 60 boost sourced registrations, on top of email and organic
* Decision rule Friday August 8: if cost per registration runs above $15, shift budget fully to the Los Angeles radius audience, it converts warmer for a local founder led brand
